About Me

Solution Architect with 15+ years of IT experience (career started June 2011), specialising in enterprise cloud platform design, application modernisation, and digital transformation at global scale. At Inter IKEA Digital, I am the sole architect of the PTAG platform — IKEA's global system for managing physical and electronic price tags across thousands of stores worldwide.

I designed the Catalogue Hub — a unified platform hub that acts as the single source of truth for all pricing and product data across the PTAG ecosystem, eliminating duplication across multiple teams and standardising how information flows to Print Management, ESL/DTAG systems, and downstream consumers. This pricing architecture alone saved multiple teams from maintaining redundant data independently.

I led the Akamai → Cloudflare migration across 10 domains, and engineered a Python-based QR Code solution that lands customers on the correct product page — both delivering significant performance improvements and cost savings.
