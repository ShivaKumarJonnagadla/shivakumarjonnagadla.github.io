About

Architecture leadership, engineering that never left the keyboard

I am a Solution Architect with 15+ years in enterprise IT, designing and delivering global, cloud-native platforms on Google Cloud where more than 80% of the data arrives through integrations. I partner with product owners to shape requirements into designs, lead cross-team data and schema agreements, and set the architecture patterns and roadmaps that other teams build against.

I remain hands-on. I own 20+ Java Spring Boot microservices and the middleware behind the front end, write and merge production code daily, and keep architectural decisions grounded in the real codebase. My depth is in Java, event-driven design on Kafka, PostgreSQL and cloud-native platforms, with a strong command of security, performance and resilience.

I am a Google Cloud Certified Professional Cloud Architect who works AI-native with Claude Code and MCP tooling, and I am known for modernising platforms and driving measurable cost reduction.
